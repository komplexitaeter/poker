Agile Poker – mockups

Static design mockups for discussion. Not part of the app, no data is sent anywhere. Older mockups (board, topic list, rail) are implemented and removed; they remain in the git history.

Session planner (B-129 / B-52) – separate mockup

  • 1 Plan – agenda as a timeline (height = timebox), “55 of 60 min planned” · light
  • 2 Maturity control – (a) lying vs. (b) standing triangle
  • 3 In the meeting – on time · behind plan, item over its timebox
  • 4 Results – proposal, “Bring back” / “Next item”, “Copy results”
  • 5A One item over several sessions – no history · 5B with passes

Planner parameters: state (plan|maturity|meeting|results|history), mat (a|b), pace (ontime|behind), hist (a|b), mode, clean=1.

Fish tokens – donate, load your emoji bar, fish blocker – separate mockup

A donation to us gives thank-you tokens; tokens load any emoji of the bar or a fish blocker. Clickable, fake data only (nothing is charged, nothing is sent). Mock tab on the left or M: throw fish at you, auto chaos, wallet presets.

  • 1 Emoji bar – free throws “1×”, quiet hint “♥ Support us · get more throws” · light
  • 2 You menu – Support Agile Poker: honest text, three packages (placeholder prices)
  • 3 Paddle checkout (mock) – Pay → thank you
  • 4 After paying – the code (Copy, Share as gift, Renew code), Redeem, blocker
  • 5 Loading the bar – tap +1, hold for more, drag the coin onto an emoji (+5); “1× +12”
  • 6 Fish blocker – vf throws a fish at you, it bounces back onto vf’s card · light · reduced motion

Fish tokens parameters: state (board|bar|load|support|checkout|code|blocker), mode, motion=reduced, clean=1, redeem=FISH-XXXX-XXXX (gift link).
